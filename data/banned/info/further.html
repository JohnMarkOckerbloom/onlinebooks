{pt:Further reading}

Websites

  • Banned Books Week 2026 is observed October 4-10. Find out more at the {ln:https://bannedbooksweek.org/|Banned Books Week Coalition website} and the {ln:https://www.ala.org/advocacy/bbooks/banned|American Library Association website}.
  • The EveryLibrary Institute has Tasslsyn Magnusson's {ln:https://www.everylibraryinstitute.org/book_censorship_database_magnusson|Book Censorship database} tracking book bans and challenges in the United States from 2021 onward.
  • PEN America has {ln:https://pen.org/book-bans/|Book ban reports} and data set tracking recent attempts to censor books in schools.
  • Extensive book bans have long been in effect in many prisons. Books to Prisoners has collected {ln:https://www.bookstoprisoners.net/banned-books-lists|Prison Banned Books Lists} for many states.
  • The {ln:https://www.library.upenn.edu/|Penn Libraries}, which hosts this website, also has a {ln:https://www.library.upenn.edu/detail/collection/banned-books-collection|Banned Books Collection} featuring original print copies of books published in the US in the 1940s and 1950s that were banned from sale and condemned by authorities for their sexual content.
